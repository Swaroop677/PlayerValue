Executive Summary

The PlayerValue model estimates on-pitch statistical fair value based on 10 seasons of verified top-5 European league data. It evaluates measurable match contributions: goal threat (xG), shot creation (xA), progressive passes/carries, and duel recoveries.

What it does not know: It does not capture off-pitch commercial celebrity premiums (shirt sales, sponsorship, social reach), contract buyout clauses, or locker room dynamics. As a result, global superstars (e.g. Haaland, Mbappé) appear "overvalued" because their market price reflects global enterprise value beyond pure match statistics.

Training Setup & Split Strategy

Zero future-data leakage through strict temporal grouping

Data Sources: FBref detailed per-season performance metrics matched with Transfermarkt historical valuation records from 2015–16 through 2024–25 across the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, and Ligue 1.

Split Strategy: We do NOT use random cross-validation, which leaks identical players across seasons. Instead, we perform a strict temporal split grouped by player:
  • Training Set: Seasons 2015–16 through 2023–24 (7,604 player-seasons)
  • Validation Set: Season 2023–24 tuning
  • Held-Out Test Set: Season 2024–25 (1,886 player-seasons evaluated purely out-of-sample)

Prediction Intervals (Quantile Regression)

Replacing fake confidence ranges with real quantile-loss models

Instead of computing an arbitrary standard deviation multiplier, PlayerValue trains two dedicated auxiliary XGBoost models using pinball quantile loss (objective='reg:quantileerror') at alpha = 0.10 and alpha = 0.90. This provides an asymmetric, distribution-free 80% confidence interval tailored to each player's statistical variance.

Known Failure Modes & Model Boundaries

Documented scenarios where statistical predictions diverge from reality

Commercial Superstar Premium

Players like Haaland, Mbappé, and Vinicius Jr carry global marketing, sponsorship, and shirt-selling power that accounts for €50M+ above pure on-pitch performance stats.

Small Sample Sizes (<900 Mins)

Substitute players who score 2 goals in 150 minutes display artificially inflated per-90 rates. We enforce a 900-minute filter by default and flag samples with a low-confidence badge.

Breakout Seasons & Trajectories

A young player enjoying their first explosive 6 months may have high market demand before multi-year statistical models fully catch up to the sudden valuation spike.
