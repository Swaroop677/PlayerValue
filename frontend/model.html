Executive Summary

The PlayerValue model estimates on-pitch statistical fair value based on 10 seasons of verified top-5 European league data. It evaluates measurable match contributions: goal threat (xG), shot creation (xA), progressive passes/carries, and duel recoveries.

What it does not know: It does not capture off-pitch commercial celebrity premiums (shirt sales, sponsorship, social reach), contract buyout clauses, or locker room dynamics. As a result, global superstars (e.g. Haaland, Mbappé) appear "overvalued" because their market price reflects global enterprise value beyond pure match statistics.

Log-Transformed Market Value

Validation Split
Temporal Leakage Prevention
Train: earlier seasons (≤2022-23). Held-out test: 2024-25 season. Strict grouping by player ID ensures no future data contamination.

Quantile Regression (q10 & q90)
Dedicated pinball loss estimators generate honest empirical 80% prediction intervals rather than arbitrary normal bounds.
